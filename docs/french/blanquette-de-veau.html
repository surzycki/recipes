Method

1

Blanch large cut pieces veal shoulder in water

  • 1.2 kg veal shoulder
2

Skim off impurities as they rise

3

Make bouquet garni, cut leek 3/4 way through to open it up

  • 2 leek
4

Inside put thyme, bay leaves, and parsely; tie with twine.

  • 10 branches thyme
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 3 branches parsely
5

Peel onion with cloves 4 pushed inside each.

  • 2 onion
  • 8 cloves
6

Strain meat.

7

Chop carrots into bite sized pieces

  • 200 g carrots
8

Chop celery into large pieces

  • 100 g celery
9

Add blanched veal, bouquet garni, onion, carrots, celery, and garlic cloves to a dutch oven

  • 25 g garlic cloves
10

Add chicken stock and season with salt.

  • 2 litres chicken stock
  • some salt.
11

Add white wine

  • 250 ml white wine
12

Add black peppercorns, juniper berries, corriander seeds

  • 2 g black peppercorns
  • 12 juniper berries
  • 3 g corriander seeds
13

Simmer covered on low heat for 90 minutes

14

Remove carrots and leek, save carrots, and continue cooking for 45 minutes until meat is tender

15

Strain and save meat, carrots, and stock.

16

Wash and cut mushrooms

  • 250 g mushrooms
17

Cover mushrooms with water, season with salt, pepper, butter, and juice of 1/2 of lemon

  • some salt,
  • some pepper,
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 lemon
18

Add a splash of white wine and boil for 15 minutes.

  • 1 white wine
19

For the pearl onions, blanche for 2 minutes and place in cold water, you can then peel easily by squeezing them.

  • 300 g pearl onions
20

Saute the pearl onions in butter with sugar and water

  • 15 g butter
  • 20 g sugar
  • 30 g water
21

Carmelize the onions (when the water is evaporated)

22

Melt butter with flour in the dutch oven to make a light roux.

  • 60 g butter
  • 60 g flour
23

Add saved stock to make a veloute

24

Add veal and carrots, mushrooms, and simmer gently for 20 minutes

25

Mix yolks of eggs with heavy cream

  • 2 eggs
  • 20 cl heavy cream
26

And mix in mixture, do not let boil anymore.

27

Add 1/2 of juice from lemon. Season with salt and pepper
