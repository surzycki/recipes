Celebration Rice And Sweet Spiced Lamb

⏱ 4 hours Main

Ingredients

1 kglamb neck
400 gbasmati rice
somesalt
somepepper
2 tbspghee
6garlic cloves
50 gginger
3tomatoes
1onion
2jalepenos
20 gcilantro
1 tbspcumin seed
1 tbspcinnamon
1.5 tspallspice
4.5 cupswater
3 threadssaffron
30 gbutter
5cloves
3cinnamon stick
80 graisins
2.5 tbspapple cider vinegar
80 galmonds
10 gparsely
150 gyogurt
somecilantro,

Method

1

Pat dry lamb neck in large pieces.

2

Soak basmati rice in water for at least 30 minutes

3

Season with salt and pepper

4

Heat ghee and sear the lamb, remove

5

Add garlic cloves, ginger, tomatoes, onion, jalepenos, and cilantro in food processor until chopped

6

Transfer garlic puree into pan and cook until fragrant

7

Add ground cumin seed, cinnamon, allspice cook for 30s

8

Return lamb to pan with water, salt and pepper

9

Bring to boil cover and transfer to oven 165C°

10

Cook for 3 hours

11

Remove lamb from oven and baste with juices.

12

Return to oven uncovered for 30 minutes basting 1 time in between

13

Remove when lamb is brown and sauce thickened

14

Remove from oven

15

In last 30 minutes make the rice

16

Drain rice

17

Add saffron to water of hot water, let sit for 10 minutes

18

Add butter, cloves, cinnamon stick cook until butter melts

19

Add rice to pan and coat with butter

20

Add boiling water and salt and bring to boil.

21

Cover and cook on low for 15 minutes

22

Turn heat off and cook for 15 minutes

23

Add raisins, apple cider vinegar let plump up

24

In butter brown almonds and salt

25

Stir in raisin misture, chopped cilantro and parsely

26

Open rice and pour in saffron water to stain some of the rice, stir gently

27

Transfer 2/3 of lamb sauce and lamb into bowl, remove bones

28

Whisk yogurt, cilantro, parsely in remaining sauce for gravey

29

Spoon half half of rice on serving plate, top with half the lamb mixture, and repeat.

30

Top with nut garnish
