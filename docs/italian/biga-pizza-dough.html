Biga Pizza Dough

⏱ 3 hours + overnight Main

Ingredients

250 mlwater
500 gflour
3 gyeast
30 gsalt
View source →

Method

1

Make biga by mixing water, flour, and yeast in a bowl until rough.

  • 250 ml water
  • 500 g flour
  • 3 g yeast
2

Cover and let sit at room temperature for 30 minutes.

3

Put in the fridge for 24-48 hours.

4

To make dough add biga, flour, water, yeast and mix until dough it tough

  • 500 g flour
  • 250 ml water
  • 3 g yeast
5

Add salt to water and mix until dissolved.

  • 30 g salt
  • 250 ml water
6

Slowly add water to dough as mixer is running, should take about 15 minutes.

7

Put dough in oiled bowl and cover with plastic wrap for 30 minutes.

8

Make balls and rest for 3 hours.
