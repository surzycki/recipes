American

Mergez

⏱ 4 hours 👤 10 servings Main

Ingredients

6 gcumin seeds
6 gfennel seed
1 cmcinnamon
6 gcorriander seeds
20 gpaprika
8 gsmoked paprika
150 groasted red pepper
25 ggarlic cloves
20 gsugar
35 gcuring salt
0.3333333333333333 cupmint
0.6666666666666666 cupcilantro
50 gmilk powder
20 gharissa
1.5 kilolamb shoulder
400 gpork belly
60 mlred wine
60 mlwater
30 mlolive oil

Method

1

Toast cumin seeds, fennel seed, cinnamon, and corriander seeds

  • 6 g cumin seeds
  • 6 g fennel seed
  • 1 cm cinnamon
  • 6 g corriander seeds
2

Grind spices

3

Mix with paprika, smoked paprika, roasted red pepper, garlic cloves, sugar, curing salt, chopped mint, chopped cilantro, milk powder and harissa

  • 20 g paprika
  • 8 g smoked paprika
  • 150 g roasted red pepper
  • 25 g garlic cloves
  • 20 g sugar
  • 35 g curing salt
  • 0.3333333333333333 cup mint
  • 0.6666666666666666 cup cilantro
  • 50 g milk powder
  • 20 g harissa
4

Mix spice mixture with cubed lamb shoulder and pork belly

  • 1.5 kilo lamb shoulder
  • 400 g pork belly
5

Chill and grind though fine die.

6

Add red wine, water, olive oil

  • 60 ml red wine
  • 60 ml water
  • 30 ml olive oil
7

Mix with paddle for 1 minute

8

Taste and adjust seasoning

9

Stuff sausages and cook to internal 65°C
